It is getting rather frightening that a lot of my friends working in
Software Engineering legitimately organize their entire work day around
AI usage resets.

Start working at 9. Burn through the 5-hour limit in 3. Have lunch or play
Genshin Impact (or whatever the kids play nowadays) for 2 hours. Burn through
the 5-hour limit again. Pop a reset. Burn through the 5-hour limit again.
Switch between Claude Code and Codex. Limit again. Grab dinner. Scroll
through Twitter to see if Tibo is about to reset limits again. If he does,
start up some random bullshit slop side project with max thinking effort
and fast mode to burn through the entire weekly limit in one go and wait
for the reset to drop. Rinse and repeat.

As a fairly traditional coder with very high standards for code quality
(and as a cheapskate), I just don't get the mindset behind this. It's all
fucked up in so many different ways.

It's like going to a Pizza Hut salad bar and then [stacking up an actual
tower of salad pieces on your plate][salad-tower] because you're only
allowed one bowl per visit, and then planning your day around when you can
go to the salad bar again. If the salad bar tells you to piss off because
you've had too much, then you go to a different restaurant with a different
salad bar. Is the salad bar really this important to you? How about doing
or eating literally anything else?

[salad-tower]: https://www.thebeijinger.com/blog/2009/11/27/capital-bites-pizza-hut-topples-salad-towers

I get that a lot of vibecoders are ready to sacrifice their own convenience
and sanity if it means they get to produce more slop. Like trying to learn
Chinese since prompting in it allegedly saves a lot more tokens than in
English (spoilers: [it doesn't][arxiv-chinese]), or reorganizing LLM usage
to [avoid peak pricing][deepseek-pricing] when using DeepSeek V4 Pro.
But if anything, this kind of self-inflicted flagellation only impacted
the vibecoder themself; we otherwise ordinary folk don't get to suffer
because of it.

[arxiv-chinese]: https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.14210
[deepseek-pricing]: https://api-docs.deepseek.com/news/news260813/#api-pricing-update-

But now things are rather different. I now have to face the real possibility
of friends not going out for dinner because they're trying to squeeze the
last bit of tokens out of their OpenAI or Claude subscription. Employers
have to tolerate employees slacking off because they can't possibly code
without LLMs in an entirely vibed codebase. Entire departments at the mercy
of AI corporations. Our own use of time and free will bound to the whims of
one man on Twitter. Many don't even have a choice as some "innovative"
companies mandate using LLMs at work, so they're trapped in this cycle
without a way out (aka actually writing code).

Maybe then the actual form of cybersubjugation humanity will endure would not
be an outright malicious stick à la Skynet, but a seemingly innocuous carrot
that dulls and deprives us of the ability to create, to work, to breathe life;
our skills atrophied to such a point, that we are no more than simpletons
bound to a chain, consuming mass-fabricated media unquestioningly to find a
semblance of the life driven by thought and creation that we used to lead.

But perhaps that is overly pessimistic. After all, any spin on the myth of
infinite growth is bound to be just that—a myth. Soon, someone is bound to
find out what the real cost of behind all of this really amounts to.

In the mean time, don't let your mind decay.
